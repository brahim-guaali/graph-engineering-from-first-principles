=== 2. Take the reducer off 'evidence' ===
  !! InvalidUpdateError: 'evidence' got 3 writes in one round (from ['order', 'policy', 'fraud']) and has no reducer to combine them
  A loop never has to answer 'which write wins?' because only one thing runs at a time.
  A graph makes you answer it up front, and in exchange runs things in parallel safely.

=== 3. The fraud service hangs in round 2 ===
  round 1 [  0.0s ->  20.4s] classify                 wrote ['claim', 'spent']
  !! the fraud node timed out at 25.5s.
  The order and policy nodes in the same round succeeded, and their results were
  thrown away with the run. The end of a round is a natural place to save...

What supersteps fixed:
  - fan-out and fan-in: independent lookups overlap
  - snapshot reads: nodes in one round never see each other's half-finished work,
    so the result can't depend on which node finished first
  - reducers: parallel writes to one field combine by a rule, or fail loudly
Where it still breaks:
  1) one failed node still loses the whole run, its siblings' results included
  2) still no way to stop and wait days for a person
